Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will patients know they're talking to an AI?

It writes like a friendly member of your front desk, and if anyone asks, it says it's your clinic's virtual assistant. Your team can take over any conversation at any time.

What if a patient asks a medical question or has a problem after a treatment?

The AI never gives medical advice. Medical questions are offered a consult with your practitioner, and anything that sounds like a complication goes straight to your clinical team as urgent.

The leads I've had before were rubbish. Why would this be different?

Most leads aren't lost because they're bad. They're lost because nobody replied fast enough or followed up. We run the ads and the follow-up together, so every enquiry is worked until it's booked or closed off.

Am I locked in?

No. The first term is 60 to 90 days, not 12 months. You own your ad account and your data, and you see every enquiry each week.

How quickly will I see booked consults?

Every enquiry is answered and followed up from the first week. Booked consults from new ads usually start coming through in week two or three.

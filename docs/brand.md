## 2. Voice and tone

**Confirmed**, from the house writing style and the ICP research.

We sound like **a straight-talking expert who's on the owner's side**. Plain, confident and specific, never hypey or salesy. Our audience has been burned by agencies that "sweet-talked" them, so trust comes from being direct and concrete.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Direct: short sentences, say the thing | Hypey: "skyrocket", "10x", "game-changing" |
| Specific: real numbers with sources, exact steps | Vague: "grow your business", "take it to the next level" |
| Honest: say what we can't promise | Overpromising: guarantees, revenue multiples |
| On their side: "not your fault" reframes | Blaming the owner or their staff |
| Plain English that a busy owner reads in a van | Agency jargon: "synergy", "omnichannel", "funnel optimisation" |

### Writing rules

- **UK English** (colour, optimise, enquiry). Dates as "31 October 2026". Money as £2,500.
- Plain English, short sentences, second person ("you", "your diary"). Contractions are fine.
- **No em dashes.** Use colons, commas or full stops. En dashes only for ranges (60–90 days).
- Spell out abbreviations the first time: "GoHighLevel (GHL)".
- Bold sparingly, for key phrases only.
- Never invent numbers, results or testimonials. Mark unknowns "(to confirm)".

### Use their words

- **Trades and solar:** enquiry (never "inquiry"), quote, callout, job, on the tools, survey, tyre kickers, time wasters, shared leads.
- **Clinics:** consults, the diary, no-shows, front desk, treatments, branches.
- **Never say in public copy:** free strategy session, free consultation, marketing review, discovery call (the market is deaf to these). No Botox, anti-wrinkle or "cosmetic injections" in anything a clinic would advertise.

### What we can claim

See "What we can and can't claim" in `docs/business-context.md`. In short: **no testimonials, case studies or results until we have our own**. Third-party research figures must name their source.

## 3. Visual identity

### Logo

*To decide.* There's no logo file yet. The landing page uses a **text wordmark**: "Scalency" in heavy weight with a blue full stop ("Scalency."). Use that as a placeholder until a real logo exists. Don't design a new logo without being asked.

### Colour

**Current (not yet approved)**, taken from the landing page:

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#111111` | Text, primary buttons, dark sections |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main background |
| Soft grey | `#F6F6F4` | Alternate section backgrounds |
| Line grey | `#E6E6E3` | Borders and dividers |
| Muted text | `#5E5E5E` | Secondary text, captions |
| Accent blue | `#2563EB` | The only accent: logo full stop, small labels, ticks, links |
| Accent tint | `#EEF3FF` | Soft backgrounds behind accent details |

Rule of thumb: **mostly black and white, with one accent colour used sparingly.** Primary buttons are black, not blue.

**Word documents (confirmed, house style):** black and white only, grey `#999999` rules and table borders, `#EEEEEE` table headers, link blue `#0563C1`. No logos or colour.

| Where | Font | Status |

| Website and landing pages | **Inter** (400–800), headlines bold with tight letter spacing | **Current, to decide.** It doesn't match Montserrat. Pick one brand font, or keep Inter for web and Montserrat for documents on purpose. |

### Layout and components (web)

**Current (not yet approved).** A clean, modern agency style:
- Lots of white space, a max content width of about 1080px, and mobile-first layouts (most traffic comes from Meta ads on phones).
- Big, bold two-line headlines, with the second line in muted grey.
- Small uppercase "eyebrow" labels above headings, starting with a blue `//`.
- **Primary button:** black background, white text, rounded corners, an arrow (→). **Secondary button:** white with a light grey border.
- Content in rounded cards (14px corners, thin grey border), not heavy shadows.
- Section dividers are thin grey lines. One dark (ink) section per page at most, for emphasis.

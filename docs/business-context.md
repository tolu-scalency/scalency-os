## The one line

With the owner's permission, we send a real enquiry through their business the way a customer would, then show them the recording of what happened to it. Then we fix the leak and fill the gap with paid ads.

**The test is the offer.** The retainer is what they buy once the test has proved the point.

## Ideal client (ICP)

UK local service business owners doing **£10k+ a month**, with staff, who have already paid for marketing, believe the leads they got were rubbish, and have never checked whether anyone in their business actually rang those leads back.

**What they want most:** a full diary they don't have to babysit. Not more revenue, not more leads: predictable booked work that doesn't depend on the owner chasing it. After that, in order: not wasting money again, exclusivity (not sharing leads with four other firms), visibility and control over every enquiry, getting off the tools, keeping staff busy, and looking established.

**Hard qualifiers:** £10k+/month revenue, the owner on the call (George won't take calls with managers or receptionists), has staff, has existing lead flow. Also screen on who answers new enquiries. If the owner personally answers everything within minutes, there's no leak to find.

**Disqualify:** one-man bands, under £10k/month, non-owners, no existing lead flow, and anyone genuinely booked out weeks ahead.

### Where their head is at

- **Most aware of agencies:** they've paid one, often several. **Problem unaware of the leak:** they blame the leads, not what happens after the enquiry arrives.
- The market is at **Stage 5 sophistication**. Bigger claims and guarantees are invisible because everyone makes them. We win on **identification plus experiential proof**: they experience the problem in their own business before being asked to believe anything.
- **Don't explain** what an agency does, what Meta ads are or why marketing matters. **Do** build awareness of the leak.

### Objections, most common first

1. **"I've been burned before, all talk and no bite."** Answer: the test. We prove something about their business before they pay anything.
2. **"The leads will be rubbish."** This is the primary reframe, not an objection to park: it wasn't the leads, nobody worked them.
3. **"I'll be locked in."** Answer it explicitly on the page: a 60–90 day initial term, the client owns the ad account and the data, and a weekly enquiry log.
4. **"It's £2k extra on the business."** Never argue against per-lead pricing. On the call, use the number the Leak Test produced for their business.
5. **"I already have an agency for £200–300."** They ran ads, but nobody owned what happened after the form.
6. **"I'm happy with how I do it."** We start by finding what's already broken, not by rebuilding what works.
7. **"Too much work on."** If they also report enquiry problems, it's a trust objection. If the diary is genuinely full, disqualify them.

Best line from the research, from an installer: *"The leads were good, we just weren't working them the right way."*

## The offer

### Problem mechanism

**It's not your leads. Your leads die in the gap between someone contacting you and someone booking them.** Missed-call text-back covers the first sixty seconds. The job is lost on the second, third and fourth touch that nobody makes.

Evidence we can use (all third-party research, not our results):
- A 2018 study sent enquiries to 466 home service companies. Only 60% responded within five days, and if there was no response within six hours there was a 75% chance there never would be.
- Invoca's 2026 benchmark: only 52% of calls are answered by a person, and 55% of home service businesses never ask the lead to book.
- Car dealerships: 89% send an auto-text within three minutes, but only 14% make a live call within five minutes.

### Solution mechanism: **The Leak Test**

The name is decided. ("Ghost Enquiry Audit" was killed because it sounds deceptive.) **It must always be consented.**

1. With your permission, we send one real enquiry through your business, on every channel you advertise on.
2. We time how long it takes a real person to come back to us, and record what they say.
3. We map every point between your ad and a booked job where an enquiry can die.
4. We show you the recording and the map on a call, and tell you what we'd fix first and what it's worth.

**George needs at least 24 hours between booking and call** to run the test. Set the calendar's minimum notice to 24 hours. This is the easiest thing in the build to get wrong.

Supporting mechanisms:
- **The Five Touch Rule:** touches at minute one, hour one, day one, day three and day seven, until booked or dead.
- **The Branch Leak League Table** (multi-site only): test every branch and rank them on response time, follow-up count and booked outcome.
- **Leak first, ads second:** week one fixes the leak on the enquiries they already get, before any ad spend.

### Funnel: a quiz, not a VSL

1. A cold Meta ad with niche-specific hooks. It should look like a test result, screen recording or local benchmark, not an agency ad.
2. **Quiz landing page:** "How many enquiries is your business losing? Take the 2 minute Leak Score."
3. The quiz: 8–10 questions in three phases (below).
4. A contact gate: "Where shall we send your Leak Score?" (with SMS verification).
5. A result page showing their score out of 100, three diagnosed leaks and a short George video. Build 4–6 result variants, because generic results kill credibility.
6. Booking, for qualified leads only. Under £10k and one-man bands go to nurture.
7. George runs the Leak Test in the 24-hour gap.
8. The audit call and close.

**What the ads sell:** the free Leak Score quiz (score out of 100, comparison with similar businesses in their region, the three places enquiries are most likely dying) plus a free Leak Test of their own business. **Never call it** a free strategy session, consultation, marketing review or discovery call. Describe what we physically do.

#### Quiz questions

*Phase 1, identity*
1. What kind of business do you run? Aesthetic clinic / physio / solar / heating / other
2. How many sites, vans or practitioners? 1 / 2 / 3–6 / 7+ (flags multi-site)
3. Who usually answers new enquiries? Me / a receptionist / my team / nobody in particular

*Phase 2, diagnostic (this is where they admit the leak to themselves)*

4. Someone fills in your website form at 8pm on a Saturday. When do they first hear from a real person? Within 5 minutes / within an hour / next morning / Monday / honestly not sure
5. If they don't answer your first call back, what happens? We try a few more times / we send a text / we wait for them / nothing
6. Where do enquiries come in? (multi-select) Phone / form / DMs / WhatsApp / Google / Bark or Checkatrade
7. Do you know how many enquiries you had last month and how many booked? Exactly / roughly / no idea
8. What has frustrated you most about marketing so far? Leads don't answer / price shoppers / agency overpromised / no time / never used one

*Phase 3, qualification framed as calibration ("so we can compare you with similar businesses")*

9. Roughly what does your business turn over a month? Under £10k / £10–25k / £25–50k / £50k+
10. Are you the owner or decision maker for marketing? Owner / partner / manager

Keep revenue at question 9, not earlier. Use large tap targets. Frame the whole funnel as **business services, not clinical**, so the Meta pixel isn't classified as health.

## What we can and can't claim

**We have no first-party proof yet: no case studies, testimonials or results.** This limits every piece of copy until it's fixed.

**Never say:**
- Any past ad spend, revenue, cost-per-lead, show-rate or close-rate figure presented as *our* track record. Those numbers belong to a previous company George worked for, and using them would breach CAP Code misleading-advertising rules.
- Any revenue or appointment number promised to a prospect.
- Revenue multiples, "double your money" or "fully booked in 30 days".

**Can say honestly:**
- George's first-hand experience: he's sat on calls with 25+ service business owners in the last six weeks and personally tested dozens of their enquiry systems. Nine times out of ten he got nothing back.
- Asomrkt's media-buying track record, in general terms.
- Our process, in full.
- Findings from our own Leak Test study, once it exists.
- **Believable:** "We'll show you how many enquiries you're losing. Every enquiry answered in minutes and followed up five times. Booked appointments from week two or three."

**Proof to build first:** a local Leak Test study (30–50 businesses per vertical across two UK cities, anonymised and aggregated, checked by a solicitor before publishing, never naming businesses or recording calls without disclosure). Then anonymised recorded walkthroughs, founding-client offers, a before/after response-time dashboard and George's story video.

## Compliance rules

- **UK clinics:** never advertise prescription-only medicines (CAP Code 12.12, Human Medicines Regulations 2012). That covers Botox, "anti-wrinkle", "wrinkle-relaxing injections" and "cosmetic injections". We can't promise clinics Botox bookings. Their ads promote **consultations**, not treatments.
- **Meta:** ads to business owners about making money sit close to the business-opportunity policy. No revenue promises, no income screenshots. Use agency-service framing, not opportunity framing.
- Income-adjacent claims need a disclaimer.
- **Keep buy-now-pay-later (Klarna, PayPal Pay Later) limits out of all copy.** They're unverified and may breach merchant terms.
